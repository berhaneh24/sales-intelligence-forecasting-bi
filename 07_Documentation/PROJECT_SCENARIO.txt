ENTERPRISE SALES INTELLIGENCE & FORECASTING

Independent Business Intelligence Portfolio Case Study
All business data and entities are synthetic and fictional.

Business Scenario

Sales leadership is preparing for a quarterly business review and needs greater confidence in reported pipeline values, revenue forecasts, and sales performance.

The underlying CRM data contains duplicate records, incomplete attributes, and process inconsistencies that could affect reporting accuracy.

Analytical Objective

Evaluate CRM data quality, assess pipeline health and forecast credibility, investigate quota and opportunity-level risks, and develop decision-ready reporting with recommendations for senior leadership.

Original Data Snapshot

August 17, 2026

Data Validation Approach

Original source records are preserved for traceability. SQL, Excel, and Power BI support data profiling, quality validation, financial reconciliation, and forecast-risk analysis. A separate Salesforce CRM simulation supports opportunity import validation and executive reporting.

The project reflects independent analytical work using synthetic data, not a production implementation for an actual organization.
